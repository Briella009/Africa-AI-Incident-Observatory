# Research Agenda

AAIO begins as a seed dataset, not a claim of comprehensive African coverage.

Priority research areas:

1. **Move beyond visible synthetic-media cases** — document deployment failures in finance, health, employment, education and public services where evidence can be responsibly obtained.
2. **Language coverage** — expand monitoring beyond English-language reporting and collaborate with francophone, arabophone, lusophone and local-language fact-checking communities.
3. **Incident-to-control mapping** — link recurring failure patterns to governance/security controls without turning the dataset into a compliance checklist.
4. **Underreporting measurement** — distinguish absence of evidence from evidence of absence and study reporting incentives.
5. **Cross-border incidents** — improve how one event affecting multiple African jurisdictions is represented.
6. **Submission interoperability** — build export formats that make it easier for qualifying incidents to be submitted to global incident repositories.
7. **Longitudinal review** — revisit records when court, regulator, company or platform outcomes change.

The project's success should be measured less by the number of rows than by the quality, traceability and reuse of the evidence.
